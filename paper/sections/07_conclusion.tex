\section{Conclusion}

This paper presented Event-TimeRAF, an event-aware retrieval-augmented framework
for explainable Los Angeles County PM2.5 forecasting under distribution shift.
The implemented pipeline combines official EPA PM2.5 observations, NOAA weather,
hash-verified NOAA Storm Events records, leakage-safe time-series retrieval,
direct XGBoost forecasters, drift indicators, evidence-based explanations, and a
frozen Chronos-Bolt publication gate. In the final 2019--2024 Kaggle
experiment, the drift-gated Event-TimeRAF variant obtains the best numerical
all-origin result with MSE 26.111, MAE 3.116, RMSE 5.110, and $R^2$ 0.381. The
strongest ungated supervised context model obtains MSE 26.185, while the full
feature-level Event-TimeRAF XGBoost variant obtains MSE 26.712. Retrieval
improves the standalone cosine baseline over random retrieval and gives a small
favorable MSE change when fused with frozen Chronos-Bolt. The M12 drift gate
also improves MAE over the context baseline with a negative bootstrap interval,
although its MSE improvement is not statistically conclusive. The study
therefore positions Event-TimeRAF as an auditable event-aware retrieval
framework with selective empirical gains, and it identifies stricter event
availability, external validation, and deeper foundation-model integration as
the main directions for the next version.
